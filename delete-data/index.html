Delete your data — Jorik: Ding Rush Runner

Your data on your device

Jorik: Ding Rush Runner stores progress, settings and high scores only on your device. We do not run user accounts or keep this data on our servers.

To delete it, either clear the app’s data (Android: Settings → Apps → Jorik: Ding Rush Runner → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the Game.

Advertising data

If ads are enabled in a future version, you can reset your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID), which stops ad networks linking new activity to your previous profile.

Request deletion

If you want us to confirm that we hold no data about you, or you have any other privacy request, write to lopimiko27@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.
