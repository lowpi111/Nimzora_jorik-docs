Privacy Policy — Jorik: Ding Rush Runner

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Who we are

This policy describes how the mobile game Jorik: Ding Rush Runner (the “Game”) handles information. The Game is published by Nimzora (“we”, “us”). Contact: lopimiko27@gmail.com.

2. Summary

The current version of the Game does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and does not send your personal data to our servers. Game progress, settings and high scores are stored only on your device.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information directly. The Game stores the following on your device only: progress, settings, high scores and similar gameplay data. This data stays on your device and is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the Game.

App stores (for example Google Play) may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics such as installs, crashes and performance. We cannot identify you from them.

4. Advertising

We may add advertising to the Game in future versions, such as banner and interstitial ads shown by third-party ad networks (for example Google AdMob or the Yandex Advertising Network). If ads are enabled, these providers may collect and use device and usage data, including the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and operating system information, and ad interactions, to show and measure ads. They act under their own privacy policies:

  • Google advertising policies
  • Yandex privacy policy

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). We will update this policy and the store disclosures before ads are enabled.

5. In-app purchases

We may add optional in-app purchases in future versions. Payments are processed by the app store you use. We never receive your payment card details.

6. Sharing and security

We do not sell your personal data. We share nothing from the current version of the Game with third parties other than what is described in section 4 if ads are enabled. Because data is stored locally, its security depends on your device’s own protection.

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to its processing. Since we do not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing to delete on our side; see how to delete your data. For requests, write to lopimiko27@gmail.com.

9. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when ads or purchases are added. The date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will also be reflected in the store listing.
